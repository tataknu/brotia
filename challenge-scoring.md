# Challenge Scoring (offline backup)

Live board: https://claude.ai/artifact/58K5UUAgFsGmES7K4LYrCj. Use this file only if the board is unreachable.

Score each 1–5 alone first, then compare. Weighted mean = Σ(w × score) / Σw.

| Criterion | Weight | 1 = | 5 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor fit | 25 | Vague ask, or we'd solve an adjacent problem | Rep named the decision; our output answers it directly |
| Our edge | 25 | Any team could do the same | Our background gives a clearly different approach |
| Result by Sun 9 AM | 20 | Ideas and slides only | Model runs end to end on real inputs, outputs their metric |
| Data & sponsor tools | 15 | Data promised later, or their tool is new to us | Real data in hand; we can use their tool well |
| Finals story | 10 | Incremental, hard to explain | Quantified impact with a one-line hook |
| Open field | 5 | Flashy, everyone wants it | Niche or intimidating |

**Vetoes** (any one sinks it unless weighted ≥ 4.2): expects a tool or license none of us can use · no data until Saturday · nothing to demo · someone wouldn't want 30h on it.

**Splits:** if two people differ by ≥ 2 on a criterion, talk about it before averaging.

**Stress test:** re-rank with equal weights, build-first, sponsor-first and edge-first. If #1 holds in 4 of 5, lock it.
