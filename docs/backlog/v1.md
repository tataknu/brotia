## The product in one line, for a lender

"Show me which of my farm loans are under climate stress right now, **why**, how much money is
exposed, and which recent disasters touched my book. Show me where every number comes from."

Chile is the primary market and Bolivia the second region (ADR-0003). Suyana and the only validated
event (the 2023/24 Santa Cruz drought) are Bolivian, so the demo shows both portfolios.

## How to read this backlog

- **MoSCoW:** **M** = must ship for the demo; **S** = should, if the Musts are done; **C** = could;
  **W** = won't in v1 (see "Out of v1").
- **Cut line:** if it is Saturday 22:00 and a Must is not working, swap it for its stated fallback.
  Do not start new Should stories after Sunday 02:00.
- **Hard dependency:** the Open-Meteo quota resets **2026-11-08 00:00 UTC (Saturday 19:00 Boston)**. The
  ERA5 baseline build (W-6) runs once, from one machine, right after the reset (ADR-0011 D3). It needs
  ≈ 9,300 of the 10,000 daily units and must be paced over ≥ 2 hours (5,000-unit hourly cap). Nobody
  makes ad hoc Open-Meteo calls before then, or after it until the demo.
- **Done** for any data story means: values land in DuckDB with full provenance (ADR-0012 D2) and are in
  the offline snapshot.

empirical percentile; severity ramp 0–1; ×1.5 in the stress window (capped); driver sentence in plain
words with the jargon on the detail line (ADR-0001 D4, D6; ADR-0002 D5; ADR-0004 D1).
