# Family: Chile disasters, agricultural emergencies and wildfires (`chile-emergencies`)

Probed 2026-11-07 (about 05:00-07:30 UTC) from a laptop, with no keys and no accounts. Test points are the 11 Chile
locations in `farms_cl.py` (Quillota to Osorno). Every claim marked *verified* below comes from a request I ran;
the rest is marked *unverified*. Scripts and small samples are in this folder. The web-search budget ran out
mid-session, so the DMC and Minagri endpoints were found by reading the agencies' own HTML and JS.

## TL;DR

| Need (recent-disasters panel, Chile) | Primary | Fallback / complement |
|---|---|---|
| **Wildfire burned perimeters, history** | **GWIS/EFFIS `nrt.ba.poly` WFS**: keyless, 2012 to 2026-10-18, has polygons with `initialdate` and `area` | **Copernicus EMS** `observedEventA` vectors, when an activation exists (EMSR715 Feb-2024 Valparaíso; EMSR859 Jan-2026 Biobío/Ñuble) |
| **Wildfire, current season / near-real time** | **CONAF SIDCO perimeters** republished keyless on SENAPRED's ArcGIS Server (`interoperabilidad/sidco_ide`) | **FIRMS NOAA-20/NOAA-21 7-day CSVs** (about 4 h latency), counted in rings of ≤5 km and ≤25 km |
| Wildfire baseline before 2012 | **CONAF GEPRIF ignition points for fires >50 ha, 2002-2024** (keyless FeatureServer, spatial query) | **PANGAEA Landscape Fire Scars summary**: 12,250 CONAF fires 1985-2018, CC-BY-4.0. FIRMS MODIS yearly Chile CSVs 2000-2025 |
| **Agricultural emergency declarations (Minagri)** | **Diario Oficial daily-index scraper** (`probe_diario_oficial.py`); the communes come from the PDF "Resuelvo" text | Minagri / SEREMI news (manual) |
| **Water-scarcity decrees (megadrought, irrigation)** | **DGA historical XLS** (2008-2026, 270 decrees), joined by province or commune | The same Diario Oficial scraper catches new MOP decrees ("escasez hídrica") |
| Drought footprint (gridded) | **Copernicus GDO Drought Tracking** (SPEI clusters, 0.25°, 1991-): finds 2019-20, 2023, 2025 and 2025-26 clusters over the farms | GDACS DR (context only) |
| **Weather warnings (frost, heat, wind, rain, storms)** | **DMC `datos_AAA.json` + zone GeoJSON**: keyless, live, has polygons | IFRC GO Chile (country id 46) for national-level events |
| Floods | **Gap.** CEMS has no Chile flood activation in 2023-2026, and GDACS FL polygons are national envelopes | Defer to the `sar-flood-water` family (Sentinel-1). SENAPRED per-event layers (e.g. the July 2026 atmospheric river) are ad hoc |
| Index and cross-reference | GLIDE (CHL), IFRC GO (id 46), ReliefWeb RSS `(C57)` | DesInventar CHL is only for 1970-2014 context |

**Best finds:**
- **GWIS burned-area polygons.** They give a keyless, polygon-level fire history back to 2012. The Feb-2023 total over central and southern Chile is 517,625 ha, close to the official season figure.
- **DMC warning JSON.** At probe time a live heat **Alerta AA193/2026** (7-8 Nov) covers 6 of the 11 farms.
- **Diario Oficial scraper.** It finds the Minagri resolutions, for example Res. 490 exenta (6 Aug 2026), which puts the *Osorno* farm under an agricultural emergency, and Res. 459 (28 Jul 2026), which covers the whole Coquimbo region (*Ovalle*) and the continental provinces of Valparaíso (*Quillota*).

## Recommended Chile event-footprint design

Use the same normalised `events` table as the events-catalogs family, so the Bolivia and Chile panels merge:
`event_id, source, hazard, start, end, footprint, footprint_kind{observed_vector, cluster, admin, warning_zone, point_buffer}, severity_raw, severity_norm, links{glide, gdacs_id, emsr, cve_diario_oficial, sidco_id}, as_of, provenance`.

1. **Fire** (polygon overlap with the farm, plus distance rings):
   - **Primary:** GWIS `nrt.ba.poly`. Do a nightly pull for the last 60 days, and a one-off backfill from 2012 by month.
   - **Override:** where a CEMS activation exists, prefer its `observedEventA` (higher resolution, plus GRA damage points).
   - **Current season:** the SIDCO perimeters (`sidco_ide`), refreshed hourly in fire season, carry the CONAF name, commune and status.
   - **Under 3 weeks old:** count NOAA-20/21 detections in rings of ≤5 km (on or next to the farm) and ≤25 km (context and smoke).
   - **Anomaly framing (Suyana):** take burned hectares within 25 km in the season to date and rank them against the same window in 2012-2025 from GWIS (percentile). Before 2012, use the GEPRIF and PANGAEA ignition points with area to build a circle-equivalent footprint for context.
2. **Agricultural emergency and water scarcity** (admin footprint):
   - Join the farm to a commune with the CONAF `Comunas` layer (CUT code).
   - Parse the communes, provinces and regions out of the Minagri resolution text (Diario Oficial) and the DGA decree text, and expand each province or region to its communes.
   - Both are **binary, declared states** with start dates (and an expiry for DGA). Show them as "official emergency in force". They are not a measure of farm-level severity.
3. **Drought (gridded):** GDO cluster membership by dekad gives the start and end of each event. It is a regional context flag; the farm-level severity comes from the precipitation, soil-moisture and NDVI families.
4. **Weather warnings:** have the service **snapshot `datos_AAA.json` every hour into DuckDB from day one**, because DMC keeps no archive. Each warning zone (area polygon, or DMC zone or region codes) is intersected with the farm. Past frost and heat events have to come from the temp/ETo and frost-heat-hail families.
5. **Floods:** no farm-level Chile flood footprint was found in this family. Use Sentinel-1 (sar-flood-water family) for June and Aug 2023 and July 2026. GDACS, GLIDE and ReliefWeb only give dates and narrative.

## Open risks

- **No historical DMC warnings**: frost and heat "events" before we start archiving must be reconstructed from temperature data.
- **SENAPRED alert data is auth-only** (Cognito). Its ArcGIS layers are ad hoc and may disappear; `sidco_ide` holds only the current season, so it must be archived.
- **Minagri decrees exist only as PDF text.** The regex and commune parsing are fragile (province- and region-wide declarations, prórrogas, accented names). The Diario Oficial site may rate-limit a long backfill, so keep the 0.5 s spacing and cache.
- **CONAF official statistics by commune were not found.** The GEPRIF ignition points (>50 ha) and PANGAEA (to 2018) are proxies, and small fires (<50 ha) after 2018 are only in GWIS/FIRMS.
- **GWIS** depends on MODIS/VIIRS: small fires (<~30-50 ha) are missed and perimeters are coarse. There is about 3 weeks latency and a 5000-feature cap per request.
- **FIRMS points** near farms are dominated by agricultural burning; without filtering they could produce false "fire stress".
- **CEMS activations before 2023-03 return 403** in the new API, so a Feb-2023 Chile activation (if any) cannot be reached.
- **Licences:** GWIS/CEMS/GDO are Copernicus (free, with attribution); PANGAEA is CC-BY-4.0; Chilean government data follows public-information norms (licence not stated, *unverified*); FIRMS is NASA open data.
