Risk(crop, stage, zone)

Each driver is scored 0–1 and weighted by how sensitive the crop is at its current stage. Climate and disaster drivers combine so that one severe event is enough to raise the score. Geography never raises risk by itself; it multiplies it.

impact = s(crop, stage) × x x = driver severity 0–1 C = 1 − ∏(1 − impact) climate drivers (noisy-OR) D = 1 − ∏(1 − impact) disaster drivers G = mean of available geography factors base = ½·(a·C + b·D)/(a + b) + ½·max(C, D) a = 0.65 b = 0.35 score = 100 · min(1, base · (1 + c·G)) c = 0.30 tiers: normal < 35 ≤ watch < 60 ≤ high

How NDVI enters

vigour: NDVI z-score against the same dates in this farm's own past seasons. x = ramp(z, −1.0 → −2.5). The weight s is 0 in dormancy and rises to 0.6–0.8 at fruit growth and harvest.

Cherry at fruit growth, z = −2.0: 0.6 × 0.67 = 0.40 impact on C.

abandonment: NDVI stays near bare soil through the dry season while neighbouring orchards stay green. s = 1.0 at every stage, so a confirmed flag sets C = 1 and the score lands between about 83 and 100.

Scores use native 10 m pixels only. AI-enhanced 2.5 m images are for display.
