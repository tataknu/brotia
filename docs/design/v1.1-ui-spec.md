# v1.1 UI redesign: BrotIA (shared spec for the four UI builders)

The product is now **BrotIA**, tagline "Climate risk · Agricultural lending". The brand reference is
`docs/design/brotia-slide-1.html`: a 7×7 pixel mark, an IBM Plex Mono wordmark, and "IA" in the accent colour.
Aim for a calm, precise, finance-grade look: lots of white space, thin hairlines, tabular numbers, few colours,
and no decoration that carries no information.

## Home: financial dashboard across all portfolios

The headline is **"multiple allocation performance"**: how the lender's money is spread across portfolios,
countries and crops, and how each slice is performing on risk.
- KPI strip:
  - total outstanding;
  - exposure at watch or high, with its share;
  - number of portfolios and farms;
  - credit actions open;
  - incidents in the last 24 h.
- Allocation: outstanding by portfolio, by country and by crop, as stacked bars or a treemap. Each slice is
  coloured by its exposure-weighted risk.
- Performance:
  - per portfolio, exposure-weighted risk score and tier mix;
  - the scheduled repayment and outstanding curve, real, from the loan schedules (`domain/schedule.ts`);
  - upcoming installments in the next 60 days with their tier.
- A portfolio table: name, country, farms, outstanding, % of total, weighted risk, tier mix bar, credit actions,
  then a link to its report.
- **Every number comes from the service, the offline snapshot or the local loan data. Never invent a history
  that does not exist.** If no risk history exists, show the current value and label it "as of <date>".

## Portfolio report (one per portfolio)

- Title, lender, country, currency and data-as-of.
- **A row of small charts at the top:**
  - tier mix;
  - exposure by crop;
  - next installments;
  - water status counts;
  - risk score distribution.

  Keep them compact.
- **KPI "Incidents (last 24 h)":** events and credit-rule triggers whose date falls within 24 h of the
  assessment as-of. Clicking it lists them.
- **The main table, styled like a finance investment table:**
  - **(a) Farm**: name, crop and stage;
  - **(b) Outstanding**: the current value;
  - **(c) % of portfolio**;
  - **(d) Risk score**, 0–100 with a tier chip;
  - **(e) Weighted risk = (c)×(d)**;
  - optional extra columns: water status, next installment, credit action.
- Table look:
  - a header row, then a thin row of column letters "(a) (b) (c) (d) (e)=(c)×(d)" under the headers;
  - zebra rows;
  - a **TOTAL** row with total outstanding, 100 % and the portfolio-weighted risk, framed by a brand-coloured
    double rule.

  Rows can be sorted. Clicking a row opens the farm.
- Below the table: credit actions, a farms map by tier with the zone circles, and recent events. Reuse the
  existing dashboard components where they fit.

## Farm detail: tabs, not one long page

- A compact header that is always visible:
  - crop, area, zone;
  - risk score and tier;
  - outstanding of the loan amount;
  - next installment;
  - a Live/offline badge.
- Tabs, synced to `?tab=`:
  - **Overview**: headline, top drivers, mini map, loan card;
  - **Imagery**: the time-lapse viewer, with the native/AI toggle and the trust labels;
  - **Water**: the five-rung ladder and the peer comparison;
  - **Risk**: the C/D/G breakdown, the formula and all drivers;
  - **Rules**: the effective rules and audit trail for this farm, with edit and revert where it exists today;
  - **Events**: events touching the farm or zone.

## Shared rules for all builders

- **Ownership:** stay inside your own folders. Only the shell builder edits `App.tsx`, `tailwind.config.cjs`,
  `index.css`, `shared/ui/*`, `shared/brand/*`, `shared/chartColors.ts`, `index.html` and `public/brand/*`.
  If you need a shared component changed, build a local one in your feature folder.
- **Contract:** don't change `domain/types.ts`, `contracts/`, `data/api.ts` or the service.
  `data/hooks.ts` additions are allowed only if they are additive.
- **Checks:** `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` and `npm run build` must pass for your files. Update or replace
  the tests your change touches.
- **Servers:** the service runs at http://localhost:8787 and the Vite dev server at http://localhost:5190, with
  HMR; don't start or stop them. For browser checks use headless Chrome (see `app/scripts/e2e_check.py`), or the
  claude-in-chrome tools if they are available.
- **Paid API:** don't trigger the farming agent, because it costs money.
- **Brand:** no other product's name or wording; the integrator's brand grep checks the banned list.
- **Text:** UI copy is plain, short and English. Numbers use tabular figures.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard focus rings and table semantics.
